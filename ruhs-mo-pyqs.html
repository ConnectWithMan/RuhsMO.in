✅ How to Use PYQs Effectively

  • Time yourself: Always solve full papers under 120-minute exam conditions — no pausing or peeking at answers.
  • Don't memorize answers: Understand the clinical concept behind each question. RUHS often reframes the same concept differently.
  • Build an error log: Maintain a separate notebook of all questions you got wrong. Review this log weekly.
  • Identify patterns: Note which subtopics appear every year (e.g., eclampsia, DKA, immunization) — these are guaranteed to appear again.
  • Review answer keys: Always cross-check with the official RUHS answer key, not unofficial sources, to avoid learning wrong answers.
