MERIT DETERMINANTS

Factors Affecting the Cutoff

The official cutoff marks for the Rajasthan Medical Officer recruitment can vary each year depending on several key factors:

  • Number of Vacancies: A higher number of sanctioned posts usually lowers the cutoff score threshold.
  • Exam Difficulty Level: If the 100 MCQ question paper is tough, average scores decrease, lowering the cutoff.
  • Applicant Volume: An increase in the total number of appearing candidates heightens overall competition.
  • Reservation Policy: Category-wise quotas mandated by the Government of Rajasthan govern individual cutoffs.
