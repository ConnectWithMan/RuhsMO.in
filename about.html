OUR IDENTITY

Who We Are

Welcome to RUHS MO Portal, your dedicated platform for all information regarding the Rajasthan University of Health Sciences (RUHS) Medical Officer Recruitment.

Our goal is to simplify the recruitment process for medical graduates by providing accurate, up-to-date, and easily accessible information all in one place. We continually monitor official announcements from the Medical, Health & Family Welfare Department, Rajasthan to ensure candidates stay informed.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

We aim to empower thousands of medical graduates in Rajasthan by ensuring they never miss a vital update. From eligibility criteria and detailed syllabus breakdowns to important dates, previous year paper archives, and application workflows, we strive to be the ultimate guide for your RUHS Medical Officer career journey.
