How to Prepare for RUHS MO 2026 Using This Analysis

A 3-phase revision methodology designed to maximize score per hour of study time.

PHASE 1 (WEEKS 1–3)

Master the 11 Guaranteed Topics First

The 11 topics that appeared in all 9 papers account for over 294 questions (~32% of the whole exam). Cover Neoplasia markers, Viral exanthemata, Diabetes, Jaundice/Cirrhosis, Anaemia, Stroke, Endocrine Physiology, Neonatal problems, and Emergency Imaging modalities thoroughly.

  • Solve 100+ MCQs on each of these 11 areas.
  • Maintain a dedicated 1-page high-yield formula & value notebook.

Consolidate the Big Five Subjects

Medicine, PSM, Pathology, Surgery, and Microbiology together make up 68% of the paper. In PSM, prioritize Contraception, Vital Stats, BMW, and Epidemic curves. In Surgery, prioritize Oesophagus, Gallbladder/Pancreas, Breast lumps, and Acute abdomen.

Common Questions About RUHS MO Syllabus & Trends

Every question from the 9 official Rajasthan Medical Officer examination papers conducted between 2013 and 2024 (2013, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2022, and 2024) was individually solved, verified, and mapped to its primary MBBS subject and clinical topic. A total of 920 questions across 16 subjects were analyzed.
Historically, RUHS Medical Officer exams have NOT had negative marking (1 mark awarded per correct answer, 0 for unattempted/incorrect). However, candidates are advised to verify the instructions in the official 2026 notification brochure once released.
Community Medicine (PSM) and Forensic Medicine (FMT) offer the highest score return for study hours. In PSM, 16 recurring topics (Contraception, Vital Stats, BMW, Screening tests) alone yield 115 questions.
