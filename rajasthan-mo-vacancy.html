BASIC ELIGIBILITY

Educational Qualification

To be eligible for the RUHS Medical Officer post, candidates must possess the following basic educational qualifications:

  • MBBS Degree: A recognized MBBS degree under the National Medical Commission (NMC) / Medical Council of India (MCI) Act.
  • Internship: Completion of the mandatory one-year compulsory rotating internship.
  • RMC Registration: Permanent or provisional registration with the Rajasthan Medical Council (RMC) is strictly required.

STATE RESERVATION

Rajasthan Domicile & Reservation Policies

The RUHS Medical Officer recruitment strictly follows the reservation policies mandated by the Government of Rajasthan. Key points:

  • Domicile Requirement: The benefits of category-based reservations (SC/ST/OBC/MBC/EWS) are exclusively available to bona fide residents (domiciles) of Rajasthan.
  • Other State Candidates: Eligible under the Unreserved (General) category.
  • Female Reservation: Horizontal reservation applies for female candidates per state rules.
  • Valid Documentation: Candidates claiming reservation benefits must possess valid category and domicile certificates.

SERVICE POSTING

Where Are MOs Posted? Posting Locations & Types

🏥
Rural

Primary Health Centres (PHCs)

Most new recruits are initially posted at PHCs in rural Rajasthan. PHC posting is typically mandatory for 2–3 years as per rural service obligation.

🏨
Block Level

Community Health Centres (CHCs)

CHCs serve block-level populations and offer a broader patient caseload. MOs with completed rural service may be transferred to CHC postings.

🏛️
District

District Hospitals & SDH

Sub-Divisional Hospitals and District Hospitals in Rajasthan cities. Typically allotted after seniority and on transfer request post rural service period.
