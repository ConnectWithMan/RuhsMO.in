    RUHS MO 2024 Paper Pattern & Subject Weightage

    🩺
    High Yield

    Clinical Medicine & PSM

    Community Medicine (PSM), Internal Medicine, and Preventive Health comprised over 35% of the paper, testing national health programs, committees, outbreak management, and cardiology.

    🔪
    Surgical Core

    Surgery, Ortho & Allied

    General Surgery, Orthopaedics, Ophthalmology, and ENT emphasized emergency triage, fracture management (PFN, clavicle), airway compromise, and trauma cases.

    👶
    Core Clinical

    OBG, Peds & Forensics

    High-frequency coverage of labor dystocias, pediatric developmental milestones, vaccination schedules, postmortem signs (Tache noire), and medical jurisprudence.
