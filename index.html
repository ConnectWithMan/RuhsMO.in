ABOUT THE RECRUITMENT

About Rajasthan MO Recruitment 2026

The Rajasthan University of Health Sciences (RUHS) conducts the Medical Officer (MO) direct recruitment examination to fill vacancies across various government medical institutions and community health centers in Rajasthan. This is a highly sought-after opportunity for medical professionals looking to serve in the state healthcare sector under standard Pay Level-14.

Candidates selected as Medical Officers will be placed under the standard pay scale as determined by the Rajasthan Government. The selection process typically involves a written test of 100 objective questions covering core MBBS subjects, followed by rigorous document verification to ensure all eligibility and NMC/RMC registration requirements are meticulously met.

Stay tuned to this portal for real-time updates regarding the Rajasthan MO Notification 2026, application deadlines, syllabus PDF downloads, and admit card release dates.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know

Candidates must possess an MBBS degree from a university recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC) and must be registered with the Rajasthan Medical Council (RMC) by the date specified in the official notification.

You can apply online through the official RUHS recruitment portal (ruhsraj.org) once the online application link is activated. Detailed step-by-step instructions will be provided in our Notification section.

The examination consists of 100 objective MCQs. Please refer to the official exam pattern booklet for specific updates on negative marking rules for the 2026 cycle.

Generally, the age limit is between 22 and 45 years. Upper age relaxations apply for reserved categories (SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EWS, and Female candidates) per Rajasthan Government norms.

The selection process consists of a 100-mark written competitive examination followed by document verification of NMC/RMC registration, degree credentials, and generation of the state merit list.

Application fees vary by category (Unreserved, OBC, SC, ST, EWS). The official fee breakdown will be released in the advertisement notification PDF.

Yes. Candidates must be bonafide residents of Rajasthan. A permanent address or residential certificate proving at least 7 years of continuous residence in the state is required. Non-Rajasthan residents are not eligible for this direct recruitment.

Required documents include: MBBS degree/provisional certificate, RMC permanent/provisional registration certificate, internship completion certificate, Rajasthan domicile certificate, category certificate (if applicable: OBC/SC/ST/EWS/PwD), valid photo ID proof, and passport-size photographs. Carry originals and self-attested photocopies.

No. Applicants must have completed their MBBS degree and mandatory one-year internship before the application closing date. Final-year students and those still doing internship are not eligible to apply for this post.
