Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

The official notification for Rajasthan MO Recruitment 2026 is expected to be released soon by RUHS on ruhsraj.org.

Candidates must possess an MBBS degree recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC) along with compulsory 1-year internship completion.

Yes, permanent or provisional registration with the Rajasthan Medical Council (RMC) is strictly mandatory prior to document verification.

Yes, candidates from outside Rajasthan can apply provided they meet MBBS & RMC registration rules. They will be considered under Unreserved category.

The age limit is between 22 and 45 years. Upper age relaxations apply for reserved categories as per Rajasthan Government norms.

Historically there is no negative marking, but candidates should verify the official 2026 notification booklet.

Application fee varies by category (UR/OBC vs SC/ST/EWS). Exact fee structures will be detailed in the official advertisement PDF.

Selection is based purely on the 100 MCQ written competitive examination and subsequent document verification. No personal interview is conducted.

Admit cards will be available on the official RUHS portal (ruhsraj.org) 10-15 days prior to the exam date.

Yes. Rajasthan MO recruits are typically required to serve in rural/remote areas for a specified period (usually 2–3 years) before being eligible for transfer to urban postings. A service bond may be required. Exact terms are specified in the official notification/joining letter.

Yes. After completing the mandatory rural service period, Rajasthan Medical Officers are eligible for the in-service PG (MD/MS/Diploma) quota seats at Rajasthan government medical colleges. This is a significant career benefit. The number of in-service seats and eligibility criteria are fixed by the state government each year.

Transfers are governed by Rajasthan government transfer policies. Initially, new recruits are posted in rural PHCs/CHCs. After completing the rural service period, officers may apply for transfer to district hospitals or urban health centers. Annual transfer drives are conducted by the Medical Health Department.

Joining timelines are specified in the appointment letter. Typically, selected candidates must join their designated posting within 15–30 days of receiving the appointment order. Extensions may be granted in exceptional circumstances on application to the concerned authority.

RUHS typically provides a correction window (usually 3–7 days after the main application period closes) during which candidates can rectify specific fields such as photo, signature, and basic personal details. However, once the correction window closes, no further changes are permitted. Always verify all details carefully before final submission.

Failure to produce mandatory documents (MBBS certificate, RMC registration, domicile, internship certificate) at the document verification stage results in immediate cancellation of candidature, regardless of marks scored in the written exam. Ensure all originals and self-attested copies are organized well in advance.
