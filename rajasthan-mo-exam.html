MARKING & PATTERN

Rajasthan MO Exam Pattern 2026

Understanding the exam pattern is crucial for effective preparation. The Rajasthan MO examination generally follows this structure:

  • Mode of Examination: Offline OMR / Objective CBT.
  • Type of Questions: Objective Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
  • Total Questions: 100 Questions based on MBBS subjects.
  • Total Marks: 100 Marks (1 mark per correct answer).
  • Duration: Conducted for 2 hours (120 minutes).
EXAM DAY PREPARATION

Exam Day Checklist — What to Carry & What Not To

✅ Documents to Carry

  • RUHS MO Admit Card (printed)
  • Original Photo ID (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport / Driving Licence)
  • Passport-size photographs (2–3 copies)
  • Blue/Black ball-point pen
  • Water bottle (transparent)

❌ Items Prohibited in Exam Hall

  • Mobile phones / Smart watches
  • Bluetooth devices / Earphones
  • Books, notes, or printed material
  • Calculators / Electronic devices
  • Wallets with extra papers

⏰ Exam Day Timeline

  • 2 hrs before: Reach the exam centre
  • 90 mins before: Report to assigned hall
  • 60 mins before: Entry gate closes
  • Start: 100 MCQs — 120 minutes
  • After: Don't leave till all done
