QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

Educational Qualification

To be eligible for the RUHS Medical Officer post, candidates must possess the following basic educational qualifications:

  • MBBS Degree: A recognized MBBS degree under the National Medical Commission (NMC) / Medical Council of India (MCI) Act.
  • Internship: Completion of the mandatory one-year compulsory rotating internship.
  • RMC Registration: Permanent or provisional registration with the Rajasthan Medical Council (RMC) is strictly required.

STATE RULES

Rajasthan Domicile & Reservation Policies

The RUHS Medical Officer recruitment strictly follows the reservation policies mandated by the Government of Rajasthan. Key points regarding reservations:

  • Domicile Requirement: Category reservation benefits (SC/ST/OBC/MBC/EWS) are exclusively available to bona fide residents (domiciles) of Rajasthan.
  • Other State Candidates: Eligible to apply under the Unreserved (General) category, irrespective of home state status.
  • Female Reservation: Horizontal reservation applies for female candidates as per state government rules.
  • Valid Documentation: Candidates claiming category benefits must present valid caste/category and domicile certificates issued by competent Rajasthan authorities.
