PAY MATRIX

Rajasthan MO Salary Structure 2026

The salary for a Medical Officer in Rajasthan is highly lucrative and is governed by the 7th Pay Commission rules of the State Government. Below is the detailed salary structure:

Salary Component Scale Details
Pay Matrix Level Level - 14 (L-14)
Grade Pay ₹ 5,400/-
Basic Pay (Post-Probation) ₹ 56,100/- per month
Pay Band PB-3 (₹ 15,600 - ₹ 39,100)
PROBATION NORMS

Salary During Probation Period

Newly recruited Medical Officers undergo a mandatory probation period as per Rajasthan Government rules:

  • Probation Duration: Typically 1 year for Medical Officers.
  • Fixed Monthly Remuneration: Approximately ₹ 56,100/- per month during probation.
  • Probation Allowances: Standard deductions apply; full allowances (DA, HRA) commence post-probation.
PERKS & ALLOWANCES

Allowances & Perks (After Probation)

Upon successful completion of the probation period, Medical Officers receive standard state health service allowances:

  • Dearness Allowance (DA): Revised bi-annually by the Rajasthan Government based on consumer inflation index.
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA): Calculated according to posting tier (typically 9% to 18% of basic pay).
  • Non-Practicing Allowance (NPA): Granted to doctors refraining from private practice (typically 20% of basic pay).
  • Medical & Pension Benefits: RGHS health coverage, casual/earned leaves, and provident fund provisions.

GOVERNMENT PERKS

Benefits & Perks for Rajasthan Medical Officers

🏥
Health

RGHS Medical Coverage

Rajasthan Government Health Scheme provides cashless medical coverage for the officer and family at empanelled hospitals.

🏠
Housing

Government Accommodation

Government residential quarters may be provided at the posting location, especially in rural/remote areas and district hospitals.

📈
Pension

NPS & Retirement Benefits

National Pension Scheme (NPS) with government contribution of 14%, gratuity, leave encashment, and provident fund on retirement.

🎓
PG Quota

In-Service PG Admission Quota

Medical Officers gain priority access to in-service PG (MD/MS/Diploma) quota seats at government medical colleges after specified service period.

🏖️
Leave

Leave Entitlement

Entitled to 8 days Casual Leave, 15 days Medical/Sick Leave, 30 days Earned Leave per year, and Maternity/Paternity leave as applicable.

🔬
Training

CME & Skill Development

Sponsored Continuing Medical Education (CME) programs, workshops, and national/international conference participation opportunities.
